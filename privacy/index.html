🔒 Bookli Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Who we are

Bookli ("we", "us") is an appointment-booking app connecting small businesses with their clients. Bookli is operated by Zsolt Báthori as an individual developer. Bookli is not currently operated through a separate company or registered business entity. Contact: bookli.app.team@outlook.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have.

Information we collect

We collect the email address and display name you register with; if you use Bookli as a business, your business name, category, address, phone number, services, prices, hours, and any photos you choose to upload; when a booking is made, the client's name, phone number, and email address so the business can identify and contact them; any rating, review text, and optional photo you attach when reviewing a business; the message and optional photo you send through the in-app feedback form; a device token used to deliver push notifications; and, only with your permission, your approximate device location for the optional "Nearby" sort in Discover, which is never stored or shared.

How we use this information

We use this information to operate Bookli's core features — bookings, schedules, revenue tracking, and communication between a business and its clients — to deliver booking-related push notifications, to process a business's subscription payment to Bookli through Stripe, and to respond to feedback and support requests. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis for processing

We process your information where necessary to provide the service you requested, to perform or manage bookings and your account, because you have given consent (for example for device location or an optional photo), or where processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, such as securing, maintaining, and improving Bookli. Where applicable, we may also process information to comply with legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Bookli uses a small number of service providers, each of which only receives what it needs to do its job: Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and file storage; Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Maps) delivers push notifications and powers the map and address features; and Stripe processes a business's subscription payment to Bookli and never shares your card details with us. A client's name, phone number, and email are shared only with the specific business they book with; a business's public profile — name, services, photos, and reviews — is visible to anyone browsing the app or a shared link, since that is how discovery and booking work.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, including while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove the account data covered by the deletion process, subject to information that a business may need to retain for its own records and information we are legally required to keep. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or logs before it is securely deleted or overwritten.

International transfers

Some service providers may process personal information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where required by applicable data-protection law, we use appropriate transfer safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses, and apply additional measures where appropriate.

Your rights

You may have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, request restriction of processing, object to certain processing, and receive data in a portable format where applicable. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. You can edit or remove your business profile, services, and photos in Settings. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete account. You can also turn off location access and push notifications in your device settings. To exercise a data-protection right, contact bookli.app.team@outlook.com.

Complaints

If you believe your personal data is being processed unlawfully, you can contact us first at bookli.app.team@outlook.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or another competent supervisory authority in the country where you live or work.

Security

Data in transit between the app and our service providers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). We rely on our providers' own security practices for data at rest, and take reasonable steps to keep access to it restricted to what each part of the system genuinely needs.

Children's privacy

Bookli is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to bookli.app.team@outlook.com.
