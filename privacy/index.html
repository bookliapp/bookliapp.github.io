🔒 Bookli Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Bookli ("we", "us") is an appointment-booking app for small businesses and their clients. This page explains what information Bookli collects, why, and how you can control or remove it. Bookli is operated by Zsolt Báthori.

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Bookli:

  • Account information: your email address and display name, used to sign you in and identify you across devices.
  • Business profile (business accounts only): business name, category, address, phone number, services, prices, hours, and any photos you choose to upload (banner, logo, service photos).
  • Booking information: when you book an appointment, the business you're booking with receives your name, phone number, and email address so they can identify and contact you about the appointment. When you're the business, this same information is collected about your clients so you can manage your schedule.
  • Reviews and feedback: a rating, review text, and an optional photo you choose to attach when reviewing a business, and the message and optional photo you send through the in-app feedback form.
  • Location: a business's address is converted into map coordinates so clients can find it and sort by distance; a client's device location is only ever used, with your permission, for the optional "Nearby" sort in Discover — it is not stored or shared.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used solely to deliver booking-related notifications (e.g. a new booking, a cancellation) to the right device.
  • Subscription & billing (business accounts only): if a business subscribes to Bookli, payment is handled entirely by Stripe — Bookli never sees or stores your card details. We only store whether a subscription is active and its renewal date.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of the app — bookings, schedules, revenue tracking, and communication between a business and its clients.
  • To send booking-related push notifications.
  • To process a business's subscription payment to Bookli, through Stripe.
  • To respond to feedback and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Bookli uses a small number of service providers to operate the app, each of which only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps) — delivers push notifications and powers the map/address features.
  • Stripe — processes a business's subscription payment to Bookli.

A client's name/phone/email is shared only with the specific business they book with, and a business's public profile (name, services, photos, reviews) is visible to anyone browsing the app or a shared link, by design — that's how discovery and booking work.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or remove your business profile, services, and photos at any time in Settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account — including your business, bookings, revenue entries, and client notes — from Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • You can turn off location access and push notifications at any time in your device's own settings; the app continues to work without them, just without the "Nearby" sort or notifications.
  • To request a copy of your data, ask a question, or raise a concern, contact us at bookli.app.team@outlook.com.

Children's privacy

Bookli is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to bookli.app.team@outlook.com.
